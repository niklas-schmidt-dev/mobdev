## Product master

Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand symbol and favicon master for Mobdev, an all-in-one mobile development toolkit.
Primary request: Design a completely new ABSTRACT brand mark. Mobdev is becoming the unified toolkit for mobile work: develop, build, run, debug, test, automate and manage real devices and simulators across iOS and Android. The identity should express many capable modules coming together into one coherent tool.
Creative direction: a distinctive compact modular monogram, subtly suggesting a lowercase m without being a font letter. Construct the symbol from three substantial interlocking rounded geometric segments, like a beautifully engineered continuous folded band. Two upward arches and a clever open negative-space channel give an understated M rhythm. The silhouette should feel unified, solid, balanced, and memorable. Soft precise corners, broad surfaces, generous clean gaps. Abstract geometry only.
Style: premium contemporary developer-tool identity, disciplined Swiss geometric design with the tactile confidence of a native Mac app. Flat vector-like construction, crisp edges, minimal optical depth from two closely related blue tones only. No shiny 3D render. Strong enough to work as a one-color silhouette at 16 pixels.
Color: rich electric cobalt blue and a slightly lighter cornflower blue for the connected modules, on a very pale cool-white continuous-corner square tile. Restrained and sophisticated.
Composition: one finished square icon, directly front-on, centered. Symbol occupies roughly 68% of tile width and 52% of tile height. Tile fills 90% of canvas with a perfectly clean genuinely transparent margin outside its continuous rounded corners. No external shadow.
Constraints: no literal phone, no computer, no cursor, no wrench, no screwdriver, no toolbox, no code brackets, no robot, no brain, no sparkles, no orbit, no infinity symbol, no scattered small elements, no bar chart, no text or wordmark, no mockups, no contact sheet, no watermarks. This must feel like an enduring abstract platform identity, not an illustration of one feature. Output only the finished high-resolution square logo.
